Textile & Pattern • West Java

Megamendung Batik

Traditional batik from Cirebon, West Java

About this art

Batik Megamendung is known for its flowing, cloud-shaped motifs rooted in Cirebon's artistic heritage and cultural exchange. Its blue tones symbolize calmness, patience, and serenity, while layered patterns reflect wisdom. Passed down through generations, it preserves Cirebon's identity and inspires contemporary design.

Artwork Information

  • Region — Cirebon, West Java
  • Art Form — Traditional Textile
  • Technique — Wax-resist dyeing
  • Material — Fabric and batik wax
