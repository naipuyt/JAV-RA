Ambang Threshold

About this exhibition

BAIK Jakarta is pleased to present Ambang / Threshold, a solo exhibition by FX Harsono. Focusing on the ambivalent position of the Chinese-Indonesian community, Harsono revisits the icons, symbols, and metaphors recurred in his works from the mid-1970s, engaging them through new approaches with meanings that are still as relevant as ever.

Event Information

  • Location — Jakarta
  • Artist — FX Harsono
